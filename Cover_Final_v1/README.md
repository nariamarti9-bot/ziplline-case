The blank white rectangle at the lower right of the back cover is the **2 × 1.2 in KDP barcode area**. KDP adds the real barcode. No invented ISBN or fake barcode is included.

The approved character art and German blurb are preserved. The translucent lower panel and other non-text design elements have been flattened into an opaque background. The background fills every bleed edge. There are no crop marks, guide labels or review markings in the cover PDF.

**Resolution disclosure:** as approved, the background has been conventionally upscaled from 1465 × 1073 px (about 113 ppi) to the 300-ppi export. Upscaling does not add original painted detail. Cover lettering remains sharp vector text.

Approval is recorded in [artwork-approval.json](artwork-approval.json). The print checks and exact dimensions are in [production-verification.json](production-verification.json). The reproducible finalization script is [finalize_cover.py](finalize_cover.py); it reads the preserved approved review assets.

The print dimensions apply to the settings above. A future change in trim, page count, binding or paper requires recalculating the spine and full cover size.
